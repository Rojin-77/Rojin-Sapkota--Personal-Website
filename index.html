Valuation of Standard Chartered Bank Nepal – Top-Down Approach

Comprehensive top-down valuation model for Standard Chartered Bank Nepal (SCB) incorporating scenario analysis and macroeconomic drivers. Projects private sector lending growth aligned with Nepal's GDP forecasts and estimates SCB's market share. Incorporates NRB capital adequacy constraints, SCB's competitive advantages (strong CAR, low NPL), and conservative CD ratio assumptions based on deposit composition analysis.
